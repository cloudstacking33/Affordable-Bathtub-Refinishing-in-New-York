Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Reglazing

What is bathtub reglazing?

Reglazing cleans, repairs and recoats a worn tub or tile surface so it looks new without replacement.

How long does reglazing take?

Most bathtub and tile projects take 2 to 4 hours of work, plus a short cure time before use.

How long does a reglazed surface last?

With non abrasive cleaners and normal care, a professional finish can last many years.

Do you work in occupied homes and apartments?

Yes. We work in occupied homes, rentals, hotels and commercial spaces with low mess and minimal downtime.
